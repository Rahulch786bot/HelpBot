# Nimbus Corp — Expense & Reimbursement Policy

**Document owner:** Finance
**Last updated:** February 2026

## 1. General Rules

- All expense claims must be submitted through the expense portal at `expenses.nimbuscorp.internal`.
- Claims must be submitted **within 30 days** of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days require Finance Director approval and may be rejected.
- Original receipts (digital or scanned) are mandatory for all claims above ₹500. Claims below ₹500 can be self-certified without a receipt.
- Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval and credited to the employee's registered salary account.

## 2. Travel Expenses

- Domestic flights: economy class only, booked at least 5 days in advance where possible.
- International travel requires pre-approval from the reporting manager and Finance, submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Per diem for domestic travel: ₹2,000/day (covers meals and local transport). International per diem varies by destination and is listed in the Travel Portal.
- Hotel bookings should be made through the corporate travel portal; bookings outside the portal require prior justification.

## 3. Work-From-Home & Equipment

- One-time home office setup allowance: ₹15,000, claimable within the first 90 days of a hybrid/remote work arrangement being approved.
- Internet reimbursement: up to ₹1,500/month for employees in fully remote roles, requires monthly submission with the ISP bill.

## 4. Client & Business Entertainment

- Client entertainment expenses require manager pre-approval above ₹5,000 per event.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only in client entertainment contexts, capped at ₹2,000 per event, and must be itemized separately.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are explicitly **not reimbursable** under any circumstance:
- Personal entertainment or subscriptions unrelated to work
- Traffic fines or parking violations
- Upgrades to business/first class without prior written approval
- Any expense without a valid receipt above the ₹500 threshold

## 6. Rejected or Delayed Claims

- If a claim is rejected, the reason is visible in the expense portal under "Claim History." Employees can appeal within 15 days by resubmitting with additional documentation.
- If a claim has been approved but not reimbursed after 10 business days, raise a ticket with category "Finance — Reimbursement Delay."
