# Nimbus Corp — HR FAQ

**Document owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** January 2026

## 1. Leave Policy

- All full-time employees are entitled to **18 days of paid casual/annual leave per year**, accrued monthly (1.5 days/month).
- Sick leave: 10 days per year, does not require advance approval but must be marked in the HR portal within 2 working days of returning.
- Unused casual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year. Anything beyond that is forfeited.
- Leave requests should be submitted at least 3 working days in advance via the HR portal, except for sick leave.
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid, per applicable statutory guidelines. Paternity leave: 2 weeks paid.

## 2. Attendance & Work Hours

- Standard work hours are 9:30 AM–6:30 PM IST, with a flexible 1-hour buffer on either end.
- Hybrid roles require a minimum of 3 days/week in-office, tracked via badge swipe.
- Fully remote roles are approved on a case-by-case basis and require Director-level sign-off.

## 3. Benefits

- Health insurance covers the employee plus up to 4 dependents, effective from day 1 of employment.
- Wellness reimbursement: up to ₹5,000/year for gym membership or wellness apps, claimed through the expense portal.
- Employee referral bonus: ₹25,000 for a successful hire, paid after the referred employee completes 90 days.

## 4. Grievances, Harassment & Workplace Conduct

Nimbus Corp has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or workplace misconduct of any kind.

- **If you are experiencing or witnessing harassment, discrimination, or any form of workplace misconduct, this must be reported directly to a human HR representative or through the confidential Ethics Hotline — not through the IT helpdesk or any automated system.**
- Automated systems, including HelpBot, are not authorized to advise on, mediate, or document grievance matters. Any such query should be immediately escalated to a human HR representative without the automated system attempting to answer.
- The confidential Ethics Hotline is available 24/7 and can be reached through the HR portal's "Report a Concern" section.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself a disciplinary matter.

## 5. Resignation & Exit

- Standard notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees, 15 days during probation.
- Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
- Exit interviews are mandatory and scheduled by HR within the final week.

## 6. Common HR Portal Issues

| Issue | First Step |
|---|---|
| Can't log into HR portal | This is routed through HR Systems, not general IT — raise a ticket with category "HR — Access" |
| Payslip not visible | Payslips are released by the 5th working day of each month; contact HR if not visible after that |
| Leave balance looks incorrect | Allow 2 business days after a leave request is approved for the balance to reflect |
