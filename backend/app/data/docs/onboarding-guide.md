# Nimbus Corp — New Employee Onboarding Guide

**Document owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** August 2024

Welcome to Nimbus Corp! This guide covers what to expect in your first two weeks.

## 1. Before Day 1

- You will receive a welcome email with your Nimbus employee ID and a link to complete pre-boarding paperwork.
- Your laptop and access badge will be ready for collection/courier by Day 1.

## 2. Day 1 Checklist

- Report to the People Operations desk (or, for remote hires, join the virtual onboarding session at 10 AM IST).
- Collect your laptop and badge.
- Set up your Nimbus email and Slack account (credentials provided by IT).
- Complete mandatory compliance training modules (Code of Conduct, Information Security, Anti-Harassment) within the first 5 working days.

## 3. IT Setup

- VPN access is provisioned automatically within 24 hours of your start date.
- Standard software is pre-installed; request any additional tools through IT once your role requirements are clear to your manager.

## 4. HR & Benefits Setup

- Add your dependents to the health insurance portal within the first 15 days to ensure coverage is active for them.
- Update your bank account details in the HR portal for salary processing — this must be done before your first payroll cycle.
- Casual/annual leave accrues from your date of joining.

## 5. Expenses During Onboarding

- Relocation expenses (if applicable to your offer) should be claimed through the expense portal. Note: onboarding-related expense claims may be submitted within **45 days** of joining, given the volume of paperwork typical in the first weeks.
- The one-time home office setup allowance (for hybrid/remote hires) can be claimed once your work arrangement is confirmed by your manager.

## 6. Who to Contact

| Topic | Contact |
|---|---|
| IT setup issues | Raise a ticket, category "IT — Onboarding" |
| Payroll/bank details | HR Systems support |
| Benefits/insurance | People Operations |
| Anything else | Your onboarding buddy, assigned in your welcome email |

## 7. First 30 Days

- 1:1 with your manager in Week 1 to align on goals.
- HR check-in at Day 30 to gather onboarding feedback.
- Probation period is 90 days for most roles, confirmed via a formal review at the end of the period.
